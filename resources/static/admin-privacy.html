Updated: May 2026 • Legal guidelines & data processing standards for system custodians

1. Compliance Requirements (GDPR & SOC2)

As a system administrator or HR manager, you are bound by data custodian regulations. All employee data processing must align with global guidelines:

  • Strict adherence to data minimization principles.
  • Mandatory logging of all administrative actions (IP and target records).
  • Secure transmission of employee PII records when connecting external REST APIs.

2. Administrative Credentials Policy

To preserve data integrity, Admin/HR accounts are strictly mandated to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) protection. Sharing administrative credentials or accessing the dashboard from insecure public networks is a violation of company compliance policy.

3. Audit Log Retention Schedules

SmartHR maintains comprehensive audit logging of all data edits, privilege promotions, and system configurations. These records are held on secure backups for a minimum of 90 days to satisfy regional labor regulations and internal audit requests.

4. Webhook Integrity Mandate

When creating custom outgoing webhooks, it is mandatory to provide a payload secret verification token. This prevents payload spoofing and ensures all target endpoints are validated before corporate event delivery.

5. AI Governance & Ethical Processing

With the integration of SmartHR's AI Intelligence tools, administrators must adhere to the following ethical usage guidelines:

  • Bias Mitigation: AI-generated performance reviews and retention risk forecasts must be manually verified. Disciplinary actions cannot be taken based solely on automated AI recommendations.
  • Data Isolation: Ensure that PII is toggled off in the AI configuration panel when running aggregate company-wide generative reports.
  • Auditability: All prompts, queries, and commands sent to the AI Admin Assistant are logged securely and subject to quarterly ethical audits.
