Quick answers to common questions about leave, attendance, and payroll

How do I submit a leave application?
Open the Leave Management module from your main dashboard, choose your leave type (Sick, Casual, Annual), specify target dates, provide a description, and click Submit Request. Your supervisor will be notified immediately.
How do I use the new AI HR Assistant?
You can interact with your AI Assistant by clicking the purple floating "Ask AI" button on the bottom right of your screen. The AI can instantly draft leave requests, explain company policies, summarize your payroll, and analyze your weekly workload. All AI interactions are strictly confidential.
What should I do if my geofence check-in fails?
Make sure GPS location is enabled on your device and you are within the designated workplace perimeter. If check-in errors persist, file a regularization request in the Attendance Tracker or reach out to the IT Help Desk.
When are monthly salary slips published?
Monthly payslips are compiled and generated on the 1st of every month. You can download pdf copies at any time by visiting the Payroll & Slips link on the dashboard.
How can I edit my personal profile photo?
Navigate to My Profile via the Self-Service navigation panel, select the profile icon, upload a new image (.jpg or .png format, max 2MB), and click save. Updates will apply instantly system-wide.
