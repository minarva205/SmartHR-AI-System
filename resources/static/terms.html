Updated: May 2026 • Code of conduct & usage rules for the SmartHR portal

1. Acceptance of Portal Terms

By signing into and using the SmartHR portal, you confirm your agreement to abide by these Terms of Service. These rules are aligned with your employment contract and corporate guidelines.

2. Acceptable Use Policy

As a verified user of SmartHR, you agree to access the platform responsibly. You must not:

  • Share login details or authentication credentials with other individuals.
  • Attempt to bypass geofenced attendance boundaries using VPN tools or GPS spoofers.
  • Upload files containing malicious code or execute unauthorized data scrapers.

3. Attendance Verification Consent

The attendance check-in feature utilizes browser-based location services. By using this service, you consent to sharing temporary geofenced coordinates solely for check-in confirmation during working hours.

4. Account Deactivation

Access to the SmartHR portal is provided strictly for the duration of your active employment. Upon resignation, retirement, or termination, your account credentials will be disabled instantly.

5. AI Assistant & Automated Features

By utilizing SmartHR's AI tools (such as the AI Policy Assistant or Workload Predictor), you agree to the following terms:

  • Advisory Nature: AI-generated responses regarding company policies, payroll estimates, or leave entitlements are purely advisory and do not supersede official HR documentation.
  • Responsible Usage: You must not attempt to "jailbreak" or exploit the AI assistant to bypass security restrictions or access unauthorized data.
  • Feedback Mechanism: You are encouraged to report inaccurate or harmful AI outputs to the IT department to help refine our internal models.
