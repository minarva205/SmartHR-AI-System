Updated: May 2026 • Legal constraints and compliance duties for HR admins

1. Custodian Status & Responsibility

By accessing the SmartHR Administrative Console, you acknowledge your status as a system data custodian. You are solely responsible for actions performed under your credentials and agree to safeguard all employee records.

2. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Administrators must preserve absolute confidentiality. You agree not to disclose, duplicate, or distribute personal identity records, performance evaluations, or salary numbers unless explicitly authorized by company leadership.

3. API & System Integrations

When connecting third-party apps, HR webhooks, or syncing payroll software, you agree to validate security payloads and token headers. Integration setups that trigger data leakage or overload the host servers constitute a breach of service terms.

4. Violation Consequences

Breaches of these Administrative terms may result in immediate suspension of system access, internal HR disciplinary reviews, and legal action if regional data security regulations have been compromised.

5. AI Administration Liability

As an administrator utilizing SmartHR's predictive and generative AI tools, you are legally bound by the following conditions:

  • Human-in-the-Loop Requirement: AI algorithms provide supplemental intelligence. Final decisions concerning hiring, firing, or disciplinary actions must remain the ultimate responsibility of a human administrator.
  • Automated Action Liability: Administrators who authorize "Auto-Approve" or "Auto-Reject" scripts powered by AI accept full liability for any erroneous executions triggered by the model.
  • Misuse of Forecasting: Attempting to weaponize AI retention or sentiment forecasting to target specific employee groups is strictly prohibited and constitutes a severe breach of these terms.
