Updated: May 2026 • Details about how your work profile and data is processed

1. What Data We Collect

To facilitate corporate operations, SmartHR collects and processes employee profile parameters including:

  • Full name, email address, phone contact, and organizational department.
  • IP address and geofence locations for biometric check-in confirmation.
  • Time off logs, leave applications, task completion feedback, and payroll histories.

2. How Your Data is Used

All gathered personal details are strictly utilized to operate automated payroll configurations, monitor attendance rates, process task backlogs, and compile internal feedback directories. Your data is never sold or shared with external analytics companies.

3. Visibility and Access Controls

Your work data is strictly restricted. Only designated Administrative Managers and Human Resources personnel can access complete profile parameters. Your team members can only view public details such as name, email, department, and feed updates.

4. Data Protection Standards

We deploy standard database encryption algorithms to guard against server breach risks. Geofence locations are instantly discarded after attendance marking confirmation and are never tracked continuously in the background.

5. AI Data Processing & Ethics

SmartHR utilizes embedded Artificial Intelligence to optimize workflows and provide you with personalized insights (such as workload analysis and smart scheduling). Please note:

  • All data utilized for AI model training is strictly anonymized and stripped of Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
  • AI processing occurs in isolated, secure containers and is never shared with third-party public AI vendors.
  • You have the right to opt-out of predictive AI analytics via your Security Settings.
